# The Shared Brain

One folder every AI chat reads first: your business, your offers, your rules, and handoff notes. Claude interviews you and fills it in, so no chat ever starts from zero.

## The promise

When you finish, a brand new chat can tell you what you sell, who buys it, what it costs and what it must never do, without you explaining anything.

## Who it's for

Coaches, agency owners, creators and people with a job and a side business who are tired of re-explaining themselves to AI every morning.

## What's inside

- `START_HERE.md`: instructions written to the AI. It runs the interview for you.
- `templates/READ_FIRST.md`: the index. Tells every chat what to read and in what order.
- `templates/BUSINESS.md`: who you are, who you serve, how you sound.
- `templates/OFFERS.md`: what you sell, the price, and the one job each offer does.
- `templates/RULES.md`: how you want work done, and what the AI must never do.
- `templates/HANDOFF.md`: where each chat leaves a note for the next one.

## How to use it

1. Download this repo as a zip and open it. Put the folder where you keep your work files. If you use more than one computer, use a folder that syncs.
2. Open a new chat in Claude (ChatGPT works too). Add `START_HERE.md` and the files in `templates/`. In Cowork or Claude Code, open the folder.
3. Paste: `Read START_HERE.md and follow it. Interview me one question at a time.`
4. Answer in your own words. Say "skip" when you don't know. The AI marks it OPEN.
5. Save the 5 finished files in a folder named `brain`. In Claude.ai or ChatGPT, add them to a Project. In Cowork or Claude Code, point your `CLAUDE.md` at the folder.
6. Open a new chat and ask: `Without asking me anything: what do I sell, who buys it, and what are 3 things you must never do?` If it's right, you're done.

## What it doesn't do

- It doesn't update itself. You or your chat writes the handoff note and the changes.
- Your chats still work alone. They all read the brain, but they don't pass work to each other yet.
- It's a memory, not a worker. It doesn't do the job for you.
- Never put passwords, keys or card numbers in these files. They're plain text.
